Functional upstream icons remain under their respective notices. In particular,
copyright.txt identifies GPLv3+ color icons, ISC Lucide icons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Native dependencies include GTK3/GLib, Cairo, Pango, GdkPixbuf, Poppler, qpdf,
libxml2, libzip, zlib, librsvg and their linked libraries. Their exact emitted
versions, source/patch inputs and license texts must be reconciled with the
Windows stage inventory before distribution. package.sh copies available MSYS2
package notices and script/inventoryWindows.py records actual packages/files.
A package notice or top-level GPL file alone does not clear the whole stage.

LuaGObject, Lua plugins, audio and GTKSourceView resources are omitted from the
Scriblark release configuration. GoogleTest is build/test-only, BSD-3-Clause;
release builds require a system package and never silently download it.

Original native, Cargo and Rust notice supplements are under
licenses/supplement/originals; supplement/NOTICE-INDEX.json records their exact
source paths and hashes. Existing notices remain intact. The source-input index
identifies the exact retained native recipes and Cargo source archives; not every
platform/dev/build dependency is linked into the application.

Modified application source, native corresponding source, original notices and
build/relink/repackage instructions: https://scriblark.trieflow.com/source
